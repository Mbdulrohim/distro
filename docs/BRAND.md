## Positioning

DISTRO is infrastructure, not a consumer app — the primary audience is technical/semi-technical people (founders, DAO ops, dev teams) launching tokens on Monad who need distribution to be correct and boring, not flashy. Brand voice should reflect that: precise, trustworthy, no hype.

## Voice

- Clear over clever. No crypto-hype language ("moon", "degen", excessive emoji).
- Confident about correctness — this product moves real money, copy should never sound casual about that.
- Terse UI copy; longer explanation lives in docs/tooltips, not crammed into buttons.
